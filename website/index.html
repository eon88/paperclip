What this is

The map your generation was never handed

Most young men today carry a low-grade sense that something is missing. Not a crisis — something quieter. A persistent feeling that their life is slightly off from what it should be. They can't name it. They can't point to what's wrong. So they scroll.

The feeling is not a malfunction. It is a signal. It is your potential recognizing that it hasn't been touched yet.

For most of human history, boys became men by watching men — through proximity, through osmosis, through working alongside people who had already figured out what a purposeful life looked like. That chain is broken for your generation. The templates are gone. The map was never handed over.

Life Compass rebuilds the map. One issue at a time. 52 weeks. A complete journey from quiet desperation to a life built with intention — grounded in how men actually work, not in generic self-help advice.
