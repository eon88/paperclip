# Social Posts — Week 35

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Time management is not your real problem. A man at 40% capacity for 12 hours produces less than a man at 90% for 6. The constraint isn't hours — it's the state you're in when you show up to them. Stop managing time. Start managing energy.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You're not lazy. You're leaking. Unresolved decisions running in the background. Unresolved tension with people you care about. A phone that fragments your attention every few minutes. Commitments you took on without really wanting to. All of it costs energy — and by the time you sit down to do the thing that actually matters, there's nothing left.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

What is the single biggest drain on your energy right now that you have the power to fix — and haven't? Not a circumstance you can't control. Something you're choosing to keep, even though it costs you daily.
