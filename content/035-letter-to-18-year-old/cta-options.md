# CTA Options — A Letter to My 18-Year-Old Self

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
Subscribe to the weekly letter. Written for the man who's still writing the story.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** "Still writing the story" mirrors the letter's closing energy — the letter is for the man who still has time to make different choices. Positions the newsletter as the ongoing resource for that man. Short, warm, direct.

---

## CTA 2 — Write Your Own Letter

**The line:**
Your challenge: write a letter to your 18-year-old self. Not to post. Not to perform. Just for you. Write down the three things you most wish someone had told you — and the three things you'd tell yourself to start today. That exercise will show you something about where you actually are.

**Placement:** End of article, closing challenge

**Goal:** Journaling / self-reflection exercise

**Tone note:** "Not to post. Not to perform." is crucial — it pre-empts the social performance instinct that would make the exercise hollow. The exercise itself is revealing precisely because it forces the reader to be honest rather than impressive. "That exercise will show you something about where you actually are" is the genuine payoff — the insight the reader is actually after.

---

## CTA 3 — Share With a Young Man You Know

**The line:**
If you know an 18-year-old — a younger brother, a son, a young man in your life who needs to hear this — send him this piece. Not with a lecture attached. Just the piece. Let it do the work.

**Placement:** End of article, alongside or after CTA 1

**Goal:** Organic sharing / audience expansion

**Tone note:** The most powerful distribution mechanic for content like this is one adult man sharing it with a young man he cares about. "Not with a lecture attached. Just the piece." is the key instruction — it removes the condescension that would undermine the sharing and trusts the content to speak for itself. This CTA generates the sharing behavior that extends reach most naturally.
