# Social Posts — Week 43

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Brotherhood is not hanging out. It's being known, challenged, and backed. Known: men who see the real version of you. Challenged: men who tell you the truth when you're drifting. Backed: men who show up when it costs them something. That combination doesn't happen by accident.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You have people around you. A group chat, guys you grab drinks with, coworkers you get along with. But no one who knows what you're actually carrying. No one you'd call if things went seriously wrong. You tell yourself you've just gotten busier. That's not the whole story.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Name one man in your life who actually knows what you're carrying right now. If you can't name one, that's the answer.
