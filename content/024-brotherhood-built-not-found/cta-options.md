# CTA Options — Brotherhood Is Built, Not Found

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
One piece a week on building the life, relationships, and character worth having. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** Broad enough to cover the full content territory while still specific — "worth having" implies standards, which echoes the article's argument that brotherhood is built deliberately, not accepted by default.

---

## CTA 2 — Brotherhood Action Prompt

**The line:**
This week: identify one man you want to invest in seriously — not a catch-up, a real recurring commitment. Training, building, weekly dinner. Schedule the first one before Friday. That's how it starts.

**Placement:** End of article, closing challenge

**Goal:** Immediate behavioral action / relationship investment initiation

**Tone note:** "Before Friday" creates a time boundary. "That's how it starts" is honest about the cumulative nature of the work — not promising a transformation from one meeting, but affirming that one meeting is where the transformation begins. Direct and executable.

---

## CTA 3 — Community Membership

**The line:**
The community exists partly for this reason: to put men who are taking this seriously in proximity with each other. Shared mission. Regular contact. Real honesty. If you're looking for where to start building that — this is one place.

**Placement:** After the section on what building brotherhood requires

**Goal:** Community membership / cohort sign-up

**Tone note:** This CTA is unusually honest — "partly for this reason" acknowledges that the community serves multiple purposes while naming this specific one. It positions the community as a practical solution to the problem the article just identified: how do you find men who are oriented toward the same things, with the frequency and honesty required? The community is the answer, and this CTA makes that explicit.
