## Email Body

Most men don't read.

Not seriously. Not consistently. Not the kind of reading that installs new mental models and challenges existing ones. They consume — articles, clips, podcasts — but they don't read. And this, quietly, is one of the most significant advantages available to anyone willing to simply pick up a book.

Here's why it works:

A book is a compressed transfer of understanding. The man who spent 30 years figuring out how to build a business, how to lead men, how to think about suffering, how to handle money — he put it in 300 pages. A serious reader can acquire those 30 years in a week. Not as abstract theory — as working models, immediately applicable to real decisions.

This is not a new insight. The most capable men across every era have been readers. They didn't read because they had extra time. They made time because they understood what it produced.

**The edge compounds.** Read 20 serious books a year for five years and you're operating with access to 100 lifetimes of hard-won understanding. The man sitting next to you who doesn't read has none of that. Same intelligence, same raw capability — completely different mental toolkit.

**The challenge:** Read one chapter of a serious non-fiction book before you open your phone tomorrow morning. Not an article — a chapter. See what it does to how you think for the rest of the day.

Then do it again the next morning.

The reading man is rare. That's why it's an edge.

— The Digital Men Letter
