# CTA Options — The Reading Man Has an Unfair Advantage

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
One piece a week. The kind of reading that actually changes how you think. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** Self-referential — the newsletter IS the reading practice in action. "Changes how you think" echoes the article's argument about what serious reading does. Positions the newsletter as part of the reading man's toolkit, not as separate from it.

---

## CTA 2 — Book List / Reading Challenge

**The line:**
Start here: one chapter of a serious non-fiction book before you open your phone tomorrow morning. If you don't know what to read, the book list is below — five books every serious man should have read by 25.

**Placement:** End of article, as the closing action

**Goal:** Lead magnet (book list) / immediate behavioral activation

**Tone note:** Combines an immediate action (one chapter tomorrow morning) with a resource offer (the book list). The book list reduces friction for men who are convinced but don't know where to start. Clear, practical, no-pressure.

---

## CTA 3 — Book Summary Series Awareness

**The line:**
Not sure where to start? We break down the books that matter most for men — what they actually argue, what's worth applying, and what to do with them. Subscribe to get them as they come out.

**Placement:** Mid-article, after establishing the mechanism of how books transfer understanding

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up specifically for the book summary series (content piece 034)

**Tone note:** Addresses the "I don't know what to read" objection directly. Positions the book summary series as a curated entry point to the reading habit for men who are convinced but overwhelmed. "What to do with them" is important — it's not just summaries, it's application, which is exactly what the article argues makes reading useful.
