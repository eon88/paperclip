# Social Posts — Week 1

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

The feeling that something is missing isn't a sign something is wrong with you.

It's your potential recognizing that it hasn't been touched yet.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You scroll. You watch. You make plans you don't follow through on.

None of it touches the feeling. It just delays it.

That's because distraction isn't a solution. The signal keeps coming because the information is accurate — there is more in you than you are currently using.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

The next time that low-grade feeling surfaces — don't reach for your phone.

Sit with it for two minutes. Ask: what is it pointing at?
