# Social Posts — Week 28

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Motion is cheap. A leaf in the wind is in motion. Direction is different — it means you know where you are, where you're going, and what the gap between them looks like. Most men live in motion. The men who get somewhere live with direction.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You're busy. You're tired at the end of the day. But something still feels like it didn't count — like the day passed and nothing moved. That's not laziness. That's what drifting feels like from the inside. The fix isn't more effort. It's having somewhere to aim.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Can you write where you're headed in one sentence — not what you're doing this week, but where the whole thing is going? If you can't, that's the first problem to solve.
