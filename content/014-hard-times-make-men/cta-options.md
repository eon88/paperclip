# CTA Options — Hard Times Make Men

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
More of this, every week. No comfort, no fluff — just straight talk on what it takes to build a real life. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article, after closing paragraph

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** "No comfort, no fluff" echoes the article's central argument — that comfort is not what produces capable men. The reader who connected with this message will recognize that language as intentional, not accidental.

---

## CTA 2 — Reflection Prompt

**The line:**
Write down the hardest thing you've been through. Then write down three things it made possible in you that ease never could have. If you can't name three, look harder.

**Placement:** Closing challenge section

**Goal:** Journaling / reflection / perspective shift

**Tone note:** The article argues that suffering is the material good lives are made from. This prompt operationalizes that argument — it forces the reader to find the evidence in his own life. The "if you can't name three, look harder" line maintains the article's tone: demanding but not harsh, challenging the reader to go deeper.

---

## CTA 3 — Community Awareness

**The line:**
Hard things are easier with men around you who are also choosing hard. That's what the community is built for. Come find them.

**Placement:** Mid-article, after the section on men who avoid difficulty becoming smaller

**Goal:** Community sign-up or awareness

**Tone note:** The article talks about what happens to men who choose ease vs. difficulty. This CTA names the social dimension — surrounding yourself with men who are also choosing hard is itself a form of environmental design. Short, direct, community-framed rather than accountability-framed.
