# Social Posts — Week 25

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Most young men who want a mentor go about it in a way almost perfectly designed to ensure they never get one.

They wait to be chosen. They look for someone to adopt them.

Mentors don't work that way. They invest in men who have already shown they're worth investing in.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You want someone further along the road to take you seriously, to share what they know, to tell you honestly what they see in you.

That's a real need. And it won't be met by sending cold messages asking to "pick someone's brain."

It's met by showing up somewhere real work is being done — and doing it well, consistently, until someone notices.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Before you look for a mentor, ask yourself: what would I offer?

What specific, demonstrable value could I bring to a man whose time is worth protecting?

Answer that first.
