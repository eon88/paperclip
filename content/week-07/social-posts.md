## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

"The mass of men lead lives of quiet desperation." Thoreau wrote that in 1854 about men who were functional, productive, and respectable — but who never once asked what their life was for. One hundred and seventy years later, the technologies changed. Nothing else did.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You know the feeling. Sunday evening dread. The birthday that hits harder than it should. That moment in the car after work, before you go inside, that gets a little longer each year. You're not falling apart. Everything looks fine. But somewhere underneath, you know you're surviving your own life instead of building it. That feeling isn't weakness. It's honesty trying to get through.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Honest question: are you building a life you actually chose, or are you managing the one you were handed and calling it good enough?
