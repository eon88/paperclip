# Social Posts — Week 2

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Potential isn't flattery. It's a physical description. A spring under tension has real energy in it — unreleased, but real. When you feel that pull toward something more, you're not deceiving yourself. You're sensing capacity that hasn't been engaged yet.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You can't describe him exactly, but you feel him. The version of you that carries weight without complaint, makes decisions without weeks of circular thinking, shows up in a way people actually notice. That's not arrogance. That's you sensing something accurate about yourself — capacity that hasn't been touched yet.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Think about the one man in your life you remember for the way he carried himself. What specifically was it about him that stuck with you — and how much of that do you actually practice?
