# CTA Options — IDEA 003: The Day You Become a Man

> **Pillar:** Initiation | **Idea:** 003 | **Status:** Ready to use

---

## CTA Option 1

**The line:**
If this landed, you're probably already thinking of one man you should call. Do it this week. One question: *"What do you wish someone had told you at my age?"* That's where this starts.

**Placement:** End of article, directly after the challenge section — or as a standalone post-article card.

**Goal:** Drive the weekly challenge action. Get men to make a real move, not just consume content.

**Tone note:** Low-friction, direct. Doesn't ask for a click. Asks for a conversation. Matches the cultural weight of the piece without pivoting to sales. Feels like a push from an older brother, not a CTA from a brand.

---

## CTA Option 2

**The line:**
We write for men who are done drifting. If that's you, get the next piece in your inbox — every week, no noise.

**Placement:** Mid-article (after the "What Replaced It" section) or footer of the newsletter email.

**Goal:** Newsletter subscriber conversion. Targets the moment the reader recognizes himself in the drift — highest emotional leverage point before the solution is offered.

**Tone note:** No hype. No promise of transformation in 30 days. Speaks to identity ("done drifting") rather than feature ("weekly email"). The restraint signals seriousness, which builds trust with this audience.

---

## CTA Option 3

**The line:**
This piece is part of a longer conversation about what it actually takes to become a man — not in theory, but in practice. Explore the rest of the Initiation series.

**Placement:** Bottom of article or newsletter footer. Works especially well if linked from social posts driving first-time readers.

**Goal:** Content depth — keep the reader inside the ecosystem. Surface the Initiation pillar as a coherent body of work, not just isolated posts.

**Tone note:** Intellectual invitation, not a hard sell. Assumes the reader is serious and capable of going further. Respects the intelligence of the audience. Good for readers who just arrived and want to understand what this brand is actually about.
