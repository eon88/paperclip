## LinkedIn Post

Most men know the difference between what was their fault and what wasn't.

Few men understand that it doesn't matter as much as they think.

Here's a distinction that changed how I think about growth:

Fault is about the past. Responsibility is about the future. You don't have to resolve one before you engage the other.

Your father's absence was his fault. What you do with the absence is your responsibility. The bad market, the difficult start, the circumstance you inherited — not your fault. Your response to it today is entirely yours.

This isn't about self-blame. It's not about pretending things were fine. It's about understanding that the person or institution that caused the problem is almost certainly not going to fix it. And if you wait for them to, you'll be waiting a long time.

The men I've watched build the most after difficult starts share one trait: they stopped using their past as an explanation and started treating the present as a choice.

That's not easy. It's not always fair. It's just the only path that leads anywhere.

Take one thing you've been waiting on someone else to fix. Treat it as yours this week. See what shifts.
