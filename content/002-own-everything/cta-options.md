# CTA Options — Own Everything

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:** "If this landed, there's more where it came from. We write every week for men who are done drifting."

**Placement:** End of article, directly after the closing paragraph.

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up / audience growth.

**Tone note:** Low pressure. No exclamation points. No "don't miss out." Let the article carry the weight. This line just opens a door.

---

## CTA 2 — Action Reinforcement

**The line:** "You just did the hard part — you read this all the way through. Now do the Friday challenge. That's all."

**Placement:** After the Challenge section, before the close. Reinforces action at the highest-friction moment.

**Goal:** Engagement and follow-through on the in-article challenge. Builds identity as a man who acts.

**Tone note:** Almost conversational. A small acknowledgment before the push. No fanfare.

---

## CTA 3 — Organic Sharing

**The line:** "Know someone who's stuck in the story? Send this to them. Not with a lecture. Just send it."

**Placement:** Very end — post-close, as a standalone line.

**Goal:** Organic sharing and word-of-mouth growth.

**Tone note:** Trusts the reader. Doesn't over-explain. The instruction is already in the line.
