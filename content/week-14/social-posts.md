# Social Posts — Week 14

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

The formation of young men was once treated as a serious cultural task.

Then the conversation stopped. Not because it was solved — because it became politically complicated to have.

The structures that formed men were dismantled without replacement. Nobody noticed what was being lost.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You arrived at adulthood missing something — not a skill, but a frame.

A way of understanding what your life is for and what you're supposed to be doing with it.

You're not behind. The transmission broke before it reached you. But the formation can still happen — now, deliberately, by your own hand.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Name one capacity you know you need to build — discipline, courage, the ability to carry weight.

Acknowledge it was never developed.

Then decide it will be now.
