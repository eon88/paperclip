# Social Posts — Week 5

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Games give you achievement, progress, mastery, clear objectives, and a community where your skills matter. Those aren't trivial — those are things a man needs. The problem isn't the games. The problem is when games are the only place in his life that provides them.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You didn't plan to spend four hours on your phone. You didn't decide to let another month pass without moving toward anything. It just happened — again. Because somewhere under the scroll and the drift there's something you're avoiding. Not pain exactly. The discomfort of committing to something that matters and might not work. The phone is just easier.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

What specifically are you avoiding right now — and what would you actually do today if you had the courage to do it?
