# CTA Options — Stop Performing. Start Being.

---

## CTA 1 — Identity Prompt

**The line:** "You already know who you're performing for. Now find out who you actually are."

**Placement:** End of article, after the closing line. Also works as a standalone pull quote mid-article above the Challenge section.

**Goal:** Drive reflection and identity work. Positions The Digital Men Company as the space where men come when they're done performing.

**Tone note:** No urgency. No pressure. Quiet and direct — like someone who sees through the mask without making it embarrassing.

---

## CTA 2 — 7-Day Challenge

**The line:** "7 days. No performance. See what's left."

**Placement:** End of newsletter. End of article. Works as a standalone Instagram story or pinned tweet.

**Goal:** Drive commitment to the 7-day challenge. Creates a micro-identity shift.

**Tone note:** Spare. Almost clinical. The brevity mirrors the message. A performative CTA would undercut everything the article says. This one doesn't.

---

## CTA 3 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:** "If this hit something real — stay close. More like this, every week."

**Placement:** End of newsletter. Below Instagram caption. Works as a verbal CTA in video content.

**Goal:** Newsletter subscribe / follow growth. Targets the reader who recognized himself in the article and wants a consistent voice he can trust.

**Tone note:** Low-key. Not "join our community" energy. This is a handshake, not a funnel. The word "close" instead of "connected" implies proximity, not transaction.
