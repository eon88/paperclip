# Social Posts — Week 12

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

The algorithm learns what you engage with and feeds you more. Over time, the version of manhood you receive is shaped entirely by your own preferences — confirming what you already believe rather than challenging it. A good mentor was most valuable when he showed you what you couldn't see. The algorithm does the opposite.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You've watched a lot of content about how to be a man. You've listened to the podcasts. Read the threads. And you still feel like something essential is missing. That's because content about manhood isn't forming you. It's informing you. The formation — the part that actually changes how you carry yourself — only happens through sustained proximity to a real man who knows you and gives enough of a damn to tell you what you don't want to hear.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

In the last month: how many hours did you spend consuming content about men versus actually talking to one you respect? What does that ratio tell you?
