# CTA Options — The Man With a Mission Is Unkillable

---

## CTA 1 — Immediate Action

**The line:** "If this hit you — don't let it fade. Write down your three answers before the day ends. That's the first move."

**Placement:** End of article, directly after the close.

**Goal:** Immediate, private action. No barrier. Gets the man doing something concrete before the feeling passes.

**Tone note:** Quiet directive. You already know what to do — now do it. Treats the reader like a capable man. The weight of the content does the work. The CTA just closes the door.

---

## CTA 2 — Organic Share

**The line:** "Most men will read this and move on. Don't be that man. Send this to someone who needs it — then come back and do the work yourself."

**Placement:** Bottom of social posts. End of Instagram caption. Last tweet in thread.

**Goal:** Organic share and secondary engagement. Creates a moment of social accountability.

**Tone note:** The first sentence is a challenge, not an insult. It distinguishes the reader from the passive majority. Either a man acts on truth, or he consumes it and forgets. That binary is powerful here.

---

## CTA 3 — Newsletter

**The line:** "This is where clarity starts. If you want to go deeper — we go deeper every week. Subscribe and don't miss what's next."

**Placement:** Mid-article sidebar or post-article footer.

**Goal:** Subscriber conversion for new readers arriving through social or search.

**Tone note:** No urgency language. No FOMO. Steady and confident — this is what we do, this is where serious men show up. The energy is gravity, not hype. Match the weight of the content.
