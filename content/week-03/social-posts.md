# Social Posts — Week 3

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

A caged animal can be well-fed and unhurt and still be wrong. The cage is the problem. Restlessness isn't a flaw — it's a man whose psychological requirements aren't being met. He's a hammer with nothing to drive.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

Your life looks fine from the outside. No crisis. No obvious reason to feel off. But there's that background hum that won't go away. You're not broken. You're a man missing what you were built to carry — challenge, responsibility, the experience of being genuinely needed. The absence of problems is not the same as the presence of purpose.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Name one area of your life where everything is on easy mode. Now ask honestly: is that comfort, or is that avoidance?
