# CTA Options — Ambition Without Contribution Is Just Ego

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
One piece a week on what it actually takes to build a life worth living — not just a life that looks good. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article, after closing paragraph

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** Mirrors the article's argument — "looks good" vs. "worth living" maps directly onto accumulation vs. contribution. The reader who connected with the article will feel this distinction immediately.

---

## CTA 2 — Reflection Prompt

**The line:**
Before you do anything else today: write down what you're building and who it serves beyond you. One sentence each. Honest answers only.

**Placement:** End of article, as a closing challenge

**Goal:** Self-directed reflection / behavioral activation

**Tone note:** The article ends with a question. This CTA converts it into an action. The "honest answers only" framing challenges the reader to go deeper than the performance answer. No link — no friction. Just a prompt that does real work.

---

## CTA 3 — Lead Magnet or Workshop

**The line:**
If you want help building a mission that's actually worth your best — not just a goal that benefits you — that's what this is for.

**Placement:** Mid-article, after the "accumulation ceiling" concept lands

**Goal:** Lead magnet or workshop/product awareness

**Tone note:** The article identifies the problem (inward-facing ambition) and this CTA offers a structured solution before the reader gets the DIY version in the article. Not prescriptive — "if you want help" acknowledges that some readers will solve it themselves. Appeals to the man who wants to shortcut to the real work.
