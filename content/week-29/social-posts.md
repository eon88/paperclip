# Social Posts — Week 29

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

The body isn't separate from the mind. Your mental clarity, your emotional regulation, your ability to make decisions under pressure — all of it runs on a physical substrate. You can't think clearly on no sleep. You can't build a serious life on an undisciplined body. Start with the ground floor.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You know exactly what you should be doing. You've read about it. You can articulate it clearly. And somehow nothing changes. Before you look at motivation or mindset, look at your physical life. Most men who are stuck in their heads are also undisciplined in their bodies. That's not a coincidence.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

When was the last time you did something physically hard, on purpose, when you didn't feel like it — and finished?
