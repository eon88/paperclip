# Social Posts — Week 15

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

The generation of men who feel lost isn't broken. The system that was supposed to form them failed. Diagnosis without direction is just suffering with better vocabulary — so after 14 weeks of naming the problem, it's time to talk about how men are actually built.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You've had this quiet sense for years that something is wrong with you. Not dramatically wrong. Just persistently off — like a compass that won't settle. That feeling isn't evidence of your failure. It's evidence that something was supposed to happen to you that didn't.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Before you conclude there's something fundamentally defective about you — ask honestly: what conditions were you given? What was withheld? A plant that isn't growing isn't usually broken. It's usually in the wrong soil.
