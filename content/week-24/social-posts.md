# Social Posts — Week 24

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Marcus Aurelius commanded the most powerful empire in the world and wrote privately about holding his mind steady under pressure. He has been dead for nineteen hundred years. He is available to you this afternoon for the cost of a paperback. Most men walk past this as if it were nothing.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You don't have older men in your life showing you how to navigate the things you're facing. Most men don't. But the men who have thought most carefully about exactly what you're going through — and who were tested far harder than you are — left a record. That record is sitting in a book. The cost of admission is your attention.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

The quality of your inner council — the voices that shape how you think about your own life — depends entirely on who you let into it. Who is in yours right now? And is that council worth listening to?
