# CTA Options — You Are Not Your Anxiety

---

## CTA 1 — Offline Action

**The line:** "If this hit something in you, start here: write down three values you want to live by. Not ideas — commitments. Then put one action behind each one before the week ends."

**Placement:** End of article, immediately after the challenge section.

**Goal:** Drive offline action. Get the reader to do something concrete before they close the tab.

**Tone note:** Directive without being commanding. Assumes the reader is capable and ready, not fragile. Feels like a challenge from someone who believes in you.

---

## CTA 2 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:** "We send one piece like this every week — straight to your inbox, no noise. If you want to keep thinking clearly, join the list."

**Placement:** Mid-article or below the newsletter close.

**Goal:** Email list growth.

**Tone note:** Low pressure, high clarity. Respects the reader's time. "Thinking clearly" matches the audience's actual aspiration without sounding like a wellness brand.

---

## CTA 3 — Product / Community

**The line:** "Most men never have a conversation this honest with themselves. If you want a structured way to start — we've built something for that. Take a look."

**Placement:** Bottom of newsletter email, or as a P.S. line.

**Goal:** Warm traffic to a product, program, or community.

**Tone note:** Curious and confident, not pushy. "Most men never" creates mild social proof without condescension. "Take a look" is low stakes — it invites, it doesn't demand.
