## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Clutter is deferred decisions made physical. Every item in the wrong place is something you didn't deal with. The man who orders his environment isn't just cleaning — he's confronting the accumulated avoidance and putting it right. The standards connect. Fix the space, and other things start to follow.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

There's a pile of clothes on the chair that's been there so long it's basically furniture now. The desk is covered in things that don't belong there. You walk past it every day and feel a small, quiet sense of being behind — of the small stuff winning. That's not a minor annoyance. It's a constant drain. And it's fixable.

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Would you be comfortable if someone you respected walked into your living space right now, unannounced? What does your honest answer tell you?
