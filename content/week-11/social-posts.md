# Social Posts — Week 11

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Part of knowing who you are as a man comes from differentiating from your father. You can't differentiate from someone who wasn't there. That's not blame — that's a structural problem with a real cost, and the first step to addressing it is naming it clearly.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

The grief around the father gap doesn't look like grief. It looks like irritability when someone corrects you. Like passivity — waiting to be told what to do because no one ever showed you how to initiate. Like restlessness with no clear object. You didn't name it as grief because no one taught you to. But that's what it is. And named grief can actually be put down.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Think about how you respond to authority or correction. Is that response something you chose — or something that was built in by an absence you never fully acknowledged?
