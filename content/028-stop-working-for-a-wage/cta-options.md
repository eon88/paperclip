# CTA Options — Stop Working for a Wage. Start Building Wealth.

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
One piece a week on building the financial and mental foundation of a serious life. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** "Financial and mental foundation" is accurate — this newsletter covers both the practical (money, skills, ownership) and the deeper (mindset, character, identity). "Serious life" is a recurring phrase that appeals to the reader who is done with casual approaches.

---

## CTA 2 — Financial Audit Prompt

**The line:**
Right now: write down your current savings rate, your transferable skills, and the one thing you own (or are building toward owning). If one of those is empty — that's your starting point. Pick it. Address it this week.

**Placement:** End of article, closing challenge

**Goal:** Financial self-audit / immediate action step

**Tone note:** Three concrete categories with a specific instruction for each. "That's your starting point" is direct and non-judgmental — there's always somewhere to start. The instruction "address it this week" prevents the common defer-until-later response.

---

## CTA 3 — Money / Wealth Building Course or Resource

**The line:**
If you want the full framework — how to shift from income orientation to ownership orientation, what to own first, and how to build the skills that compound — we put it together here.

**Placement:** Mid-article, after establishing the core distinction between wages and wealth

**Goal:** Course, resource, or financial education product

**Tone note:** "The full framework" acknowledges that the article gives the concept but not the complete execution plan. "What to own first" and "skills that compound" are the specific questions a reader has after understanding the concept. Positions the resource as the answer to the natural next question.
