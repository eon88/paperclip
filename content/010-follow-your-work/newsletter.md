# Newsletter — Why "Follow Your Passion" Is Terrible Advice

> **Issue:** 010 | **Format:** The Digital Men Letter

---

## Subject Lines
- **Direct:** The wrong order is why you feel stuck
- **Curiosity:** What if passion isn't the starting point?
- **Personal:** I was waiting for a feeling that was never going to show up

**Preview text:** You're not supposed to find your passion. You're supposed to build it.

---

## Email Body

Most men have the sequence backwards.

They're waiting to feel passionate before they commit to work. But passion doesn't come first. It comes after you've put real time into something. After you've built skill. After you've gotten good enough to see what you're actually capable of.

Cal Newport calls it the craftsman mindset. Don't ask what the world offers you. Ask what you can offer the world. Build something rare. Get good at something hard. The passion grows out of the mastery — not the other way around.

Every year you wait is a year of compounding skill you'll never get back. The man who starts building at 21 isn't one year ahead of the man who starts at 22. He's exponentially ahead, because competence unlocks opportunities that mediocrity can't access.

You don't need a calling to start. You need a direction.

Pick something that requires real skill, connects you to people you respect, and is available right now. Not the perfect option — the available one. You adjust as you go. But you have to move first.

The clarity doesn't come before the action. It comes from it.

**This week's challenge:** Name one real, marketable skill you could begin developing right now. Then do one concrete thing to start — today. Not tomorrow. Today.

Stop waiting to feel it. Start building it.

— The Digital Men Letter
