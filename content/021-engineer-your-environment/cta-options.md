# CTA Options — Your Environment Is Deciding Your Future

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
One piece a week, designed to give you something worth adding to your environment. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** Self-referential in a light way — the newsletter is positioned as a positive environmental input, consistent with the article's argument. "Worth adding" ties back to the design principles without being heavy-handed.

---

## CTA 2 — Environment Audit Prompt

**The line:**
Today's challenge: walk through your phone, your physical space, and your calendar. Write down the three things in each that are working against the man you're trying to become. Then remove or redesign one of them before tomorrow.

**Placement:** End of article, closing challenge

**Goal:** Immediate behavioral action / self-directed audit

**Tone note:** Concrete, specific, and time-bound. "Before tomorrow" creates urgency without being aggressive. Covering three environments (phone, physical, calendar) mirrors the article's framework and gives the reader a complete starting point. No link needed — this stands alone as the action step.

---

## CTA 3 — Community or Cohort Awareness

**The line:**
Your social environment is the hardest part of this to change alone. If you want to be around men who are actually building — who raise the standard by proximity — that's what this community is for.

**Placement:** After the "social environment" section of the article

**Goal:** Community membership / cohort awareness

**Tone note:** Addresses the social dimension specifically because it's the one most people can't solve with individual action. The community offer is positioned as the solution to the hardest part of the article's challenge — not a generic upsell, but a targeted answer to a specific problem the reader has just recognized.
