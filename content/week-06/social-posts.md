# Social Posts — Week 6

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

A man doesn't lose himself in one bad decision. He loses himself in a thousand reasonable ones — every accommodation, every deferred opinion, every thing he stopped building because it wasn't worth the friction. Comfortable. Corrosive.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You used to have opinions you'd defend. Now you mostly go quiet. You used to be building something. Now you mostly maintain. You used to know what you stood for. None of those individual shifts felt dramatic. Each one seemed like the reasonable, mature thing to do. But added together, they replaced the man who was actually there.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

What's one thing you used to care about, pursue, or believe that you've quietly let go — and if you're honest, you let go because it was easier than holding on?
