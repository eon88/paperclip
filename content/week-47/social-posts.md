# Social Posts — Week 47

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

You don't need to be at the top of the mountain to help someone three thousand feet below you. You just need to be three thousand feet above them and willing to turn around. Waiting until you're an authority is how men postpone contribution for their entire lives.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You tell yourself you'll give back when you've made it. You'll help others once you have it figured out. You'll be generous when you're more stable. It sounds like responsibility. But you've been saying some version of that for years — and "ready" keeps moving. Something worth giving is already in your hands right now.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

What is one thing you've learned or built this year that someone who's three steps behind you actually needs? What's stopping you from giving it to them now?
