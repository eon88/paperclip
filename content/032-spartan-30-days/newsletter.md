## Email Body

Here's the premise:

You are capable of more than your current default life reveals.

Most men never find out how much more — because they never systematically stress-test their capacity. They have soft habits, soft mornings, and soft evenings, and they measure themselves by what that soft life produces. Which is usually less than they're capable of.

The Spartan Reset is an attempt to change that. Not permanently — 30 days. Long enough to break the soft patterns, build some new ones, and discover something about yourself that comfort can't reveal.

**The protocol:**
- Train every day. At minimum: 30 minutes of hard physical effort
- No porn. No exceptions
- No alcohol. No exceptions
- No passive social media scrolling
- Cold shower every morning
- Read for 30 minutes every day (not social media — a book)
- Journal for 10 minutes every morning (what you're grateful for + what you're building)
- In bed by 10:30pm. Phone out of the bedroom
- One hard conversation you've been avoiding — schedule and have it in the first two weeks

**The goal is not perfection.** It's consistency. And it's not about the specific habits — it's about proving to yourself that you can choose hard things on purpose, consistently, for a meaningful period of time.

Men who finish this report the same things: they feel more capable. More self-respectful. More like themselves. The confidence isn't borrowed from anyone. It's built from the inside.

Start Monday. Or start today. The day doesn't matter. Starting does.

— The Digital Men Letter
