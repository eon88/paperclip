# CTA Options — The Man You Will Become Is Watching

---

## CTA 1 — Behavioral Action

**The line:** "Pick one area. Commit to seven days. See who you're becoming."

**Placement:** End of article (Challenge section) / End of newsletter.

**Goal:** Drive immediate behavioral action. Turn the reader from consumer to participant.

**Tone note:** No hype. No "change your life." Just a clean, direct assignment. The simplicity is the authority — it says: this is doable, and you have no excuse not to start today.

---

## CTA 2 — Organic Sharing

**The line:** "If this hit, share it with one man who needs to hear it."

**Placement:** Below the Instagram caption / End of Twitter thread.

**Goal:** Organic reach through personal endorsement.

**Tone note:** Peer-to-peer energy. Doesn't feel like marketing — feels like one man passing something useful to another. Fits the brand's sharp-older-brother voice exactly.

---

## CTA 3 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:** "Every week, one idea to help you build the man you're becoming. Subscribe and stay in it."

**Placement:** Mid-article pull / Social bio / LinkedIn post footer.

**Goal:** Newsletter list growth. Catches the reader at peak engagement.

**Tone note:** No promises of transformation. No "join thousands of men." Just a simple, honest value statement. Grounded. Matter-of-fact.
