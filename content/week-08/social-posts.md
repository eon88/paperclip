# Social Posts — Week 8

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

For most of human history, boys became men by being in proximity to men doing men's work. They absorbed not just skills but attitudes — how to handle failure, how to show up on bad days, what it looks like to bear weight without collapsing. That happened through hours and proximity. Not curriculum. And when work left the home, that transmission ended.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You grew up without the template. No one sat with you long enough to show you what a man navigating real difficulty actually looks like up close — how he makes decisions, how he handles failure, how he carries himself when things don't go the way he planned. You weren't lazy or weak. You just weren't shown. That's the specific thing that went missing for your generation.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Name one man you know personally who carries himself the way you want to. When did you last spend actual time around him — and what's stopping you from doing that deliberately?
