# Social Posts — Week 27

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Direction without destination is just drift wearing a better costume.

Most men know they want to be better. Few have a clear, specific picture of who they're actually trying to become.

That clarity is the difference between growth and motion.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You resist building a clear picture of who you're becoming because once you build it, you've admitted your current self falls short.

And that gap is demanding.

But the vagueness is more comfortable than it is useful. The man who stays vague about who he's trying to become mostly stays who he is.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Write one paragraph describing the specific man you are trying to become.

Concrete enough that you could recognize him if you met him.

Not aspirations — a picture.
