# Social Posts — Week 48

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Saying no to something good so you can say yes to the right thing is not selfishness — it's discernment. The most impactful men in any community are not the ones who say yes to everything. They're the ones who commit to a few things and do them consistently for years.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You want to contribute. You feel the pull toward service, toward your community, toward being useful. So you say yes — to this cause, and this one, and this one. You're busy. You feel good about it for a while. Then one day you realize you're exhausted, you're doing nothing well, and the thing you actually set out to build has been quietly starving. That's not service. That's drift.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

What community commitment are you currently keeping out of guilt rather than genuine calling? What would happen if you gave that time back to your mission instead?
