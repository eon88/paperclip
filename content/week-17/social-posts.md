# Social Posts — Week 17

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Capacity is not discovered. It's built. The men who look naturally disciplined, naturally courageous, naturally steady — almost always have a history of doing it badly before doing it well. What you're observing as talent is the result of reps you didn't see.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You're working hard and nothing seems to be changing yet. The weight still feels heavy. You don't feel stronger. You're wondering if it's worth continuing. That phase — the one where nothing seems to be happening — is where the growth actually lives. Most men quit right before the adaptation shows up.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Name one area of your life where you feel genuinely underdeveloped. Not vaguely — specifically. Now ask: have you been taking actual reps in that area, or have you been working hard somewhere else and hoping it transfers?
