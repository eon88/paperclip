## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Raw masculinity without cultivation is destructive.

Cultivated masculinity is what the world needs more of.

The problem is the cultivation step got dropped out of the process — not through malice, but through forgetting.

Not because you were broken. Because no one knew how to develop what you had into something useful.

That's not your fault. But the cultivation is now your job.
