# Social Posts — Week 30

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

The first thing you do in the morning is either an act of authority or an act of surrender. The men who build serious things almost universally win the morning before the day's demands arrive. Start reactive and you spend the rest of the day catching up.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You pick up your phone before you're fully awake. By the time you're out of bed, you've already been pulled into someone else's world — their posts, their news, their urgency. You started the day in reaction mode and you haven't even made coffee yet. That pattern is costing you more than you realize.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

What is the very first thing you do every morning — before you even stand up? Be honest. Now ask yourself: is that the action of a man in control of his life?
