# Social Posts — Week 20

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Not all suffering is good for you. Suffering that builds is purposeful, recoverable, and changes you in the direction of capacity. Suffering that breaks is purposeless, unrecoverable, and degrades you over time. The goal is not maximum suffering. It's maximum growth. Those are not always the same direction.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You've been grinding for a long time and calling it discipline. But there's a specific exhaustion that's different from healthy tiredness — it's the exhaustion of deteriorating, not growing. Deep down you know the difference. The question is whether you'll admit it to yourself.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Look at the hardest thing in your life right now. Be honest: is it building something in you, or is it depleting you without producing anything? If it's the second — what are you getting out of staying in it?
