# Social Posts — Week 44

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

You don't become reliable by declaring yourself reliable. You become reliable by showing up consistently, over time, in ways that cost you something. Keeping your word when breaking it would be easier. Following up on things people told you weeks ago. That pattern, repeated, becomes a reputation.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You want to be someone people can count on. But when someone close to you is struggling, you don't always know what to do — so you wait for them to ask. You tell yourself you'd be there in a heartbeat. And you would. But the men who matter most don't wait to be asked. They notice, and they show up.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Think of someone in your life who is carrying something hard right now. When was the last time you reached out — not with advice, just with presence?
