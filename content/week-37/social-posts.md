# Social Posts — Week 37

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Suffering that has been processed — not buried, not performed, but actually worked through — produces a kind of authority that credentials never can. The counselor who reached you knew something from the inside. That's what you felt. Your hardest chapters may be your most valuable ones.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You wish you hadn't been through what you've been through. But you notice that when someone is in the middle of it — that thing you know from the inside — you can speak to them in a way that actually lands. You're not guessing. You're not theorizing. You've been there. That's not a small thing. That might be the point.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

What is one thing you've been through that you now understand from the inside — and who is currently in the middle of something similar, waiting for exactly what you could offer?
