# Social Posts — Week 9

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

A template isn't a role model in the inspirational sense. It's watching a real man, over time, in ordinary circumstances — how he handles failure, conflict, pressure, and loss. That picture builds inside you and becomes the model you navigate by. Without it, you're steering without a reference point.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You feel it in specific moments. A conflict you don't know how to handle without either backing down completely or going too hard. A failure you can't figure out how to process and move past. A moment where you need to lead and you feel like you have no idea what you're doing. Those aren't character flaws. Those are gaps — left by shelves that were never stocked.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Name one specific situation you consistently don't handle well. Now name one man you know personally who handles it well. What's actually stopping you from being around him more?
