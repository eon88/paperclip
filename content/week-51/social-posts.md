# Social Posts — Week 51

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Don't measure whether you hit your goals. A man who hit $140k while building strong foundations may have had a better year than the man who hit $200k while burning relationships and running on empty. Measure character, direction, relationships, health, and contribution. That's the actual scoreboard.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You get to December and you're not sure how you feel about the year. Some things went well. Some didn't. But the whole thing feels like it happened to you rather than something you built. That's what happens when you don't measure. You can't improve what you can't see — and what most men can't see clearly is themselves.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Set aside three hours this week for a real year-end review — not goals, not resolutions, but an honest accounting of who you were and how you lived. Most men won't do this. The ones who do build the next year instead of stumbling into it.
