# Social Posts — Week 34

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Your values are not what you say they are. They're what you do consistently. The gap between stated values and actual behavior is where most men's self-deception lives. You can't close a gap you've convinced yourself doesn't exist.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

"I'll start Monday." "That relationship is fine." "I'm almost ready." You've said some version of these so many times they don't even register as lies anymore. They feel like patience or wisdom. They're not. They're the mechanism keeping everything exactly where it is.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

What is one true thing about your current situation that you've been avoiding saying clearly — not to anyone else, just to yourself? Name it. Out loud or on paper. Right now.
