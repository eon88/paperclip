# Social Posts — Week 10

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Reading about courage is not the same as having watched courage demonstrated, repeatedly, by someone you trusted, in a moment where it was real and costly. One is information. The other is nervous system calibration. That's what osmosis did. That's what no article can fully replicate.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You've read about how to handle hard conversations, how to take criticism, how to carry yourself under pressure. You know the ideas. But when the actual moment comes, something still freezes. That's not ignorance. That's missing absorption — the thing that only happens by watching a real man you trusted actually do those things, under real conditions, over time.
