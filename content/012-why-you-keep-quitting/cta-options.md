# CTA Options — Why You Keep Quitting

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
If this changed how you think about quitting, the letter goes deeper every week. One piece. No noise. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article, below the closing challenge

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** Short, confident, not over-selling. The article does the work — the CTA just offers more of the same. "No noise" is important language for a reader who is already drowning in content.

---

## CTA 2 — Reflection Exercise

**The line:**
Before you move on — write down the one thing you've quit in the last 12 months that you actually cared about. Then write one sentence: what was the real reason you stopped? Be honest. That's the work.

**Placement:** Immediately after the closing paragraph, before any links

**Goal:** Behavioral activation / journaling prompt

**Tone note:** Demanding but not harsh. The reader has just been given a new frame for quitting — this prompt converts the insight into self-diagnosis in real time. Forces engagement with the idea rather than passive reading.

---

## CTA 3 — Community / Cohort Awareness

**The line:**
The hardest part of staying in the game is having no one around who's playing at the same level. That's what the community is for. Come find your people.

**Placement:** Mid-article, after the "meaning deficit" concept is established

**Goal:** Community or cohort sign-up / awareness

**Tone note:** Addresses the social component of quitting — the isolation that accelerates it. Not framed as accountability (which sounds soft) but as finding men operating at the same level. Appeals to the competitive instinct and the brotherhood need simultaneously.
