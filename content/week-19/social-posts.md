# Social Posts — Week 19

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Validation and being needed are not the same thing. Validation is about your ego. Being needed is about something outside yourself. Men's wellbeing is tightly correlated with the second — not the first. No amount of affirmation fills what genuine need creates.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You have a phone full of likes and a life that feels hollow. You're not ungrateful. Your wiring is registering something accurately: you have validation in abundance, but you're not actually needed by anyone or anything. Those are different containers, and only one of them runs deep.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Name one person or one commitment in your life where your absence would cause a real cost — where something would actually fail if you didn't show up. If you can't name one, that's your most important information right now.
