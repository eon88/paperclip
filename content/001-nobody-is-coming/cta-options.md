# CTA Options — Nobody Is Coming

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
If this landed, there's more where it came from. The newsletter goes deeper — one piece, once a week, no noise.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** The article ends with weight. This CTA steps back and makes a quiet, low-pressure offer. "No noise" mirrors the article's rejection of hype. The reader just had a serious conversation; this invites him to keep having it.

---

## CTA 2 — Reflection Prompt

**The line:**
Before you move on — what's the one thing you've been waiting for permission to do?

**Placement:** End of article (below the main text, above any links)

**Goal:** Reflection prompt

**Tone note:** He's sober and considering. This doesn't push him anywhere — it holds the space the article just created. The word "permission" echoes the article's core tension. No link. No ask. Just the question, which is the most honest thing you can offer a man who just read this.

---

## CTA 3 — Lead Magnet / Product Awareness

**The line:**
Most men know what they need to do. The harder problem is building the daily structure that makes doing it inevitable. That's what this is about.

**Placement:** Mid-article — best placed after the first major point lands, before the conclusion

**Goal:** Product/lead magnet awareness (e.g., daily discipline guide or clarity framework)

**Tone note:** The article earns this because it names a real problem without solving the practical layer. This CTA doesn't promise transformation — it promises structure, which is the honest next step. "Inevitable" is the antithesis of waiting, which is exactly what the article argues against. Feels like a natural extension, not an interruption.
