# Social Posts — Week 40

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Purpose is not the perfecting of the self.

Purpose is the use of the self — in service of something that matters beyond your own development, your own comfort, your own reputation.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You've been building toward something — but if you're honest, most of it has been building toward yourself.

More discipline. More clarity. More capacity.

At some point the question changes: what are you building that will still be there when you're gone?

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Name one thing you are currently building that will matter beyond your own life.

If you can't name one — that's your answer about what to start.
