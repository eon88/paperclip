# Social Posts — Week 4

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

"Further by now" assumes there's a race with a clear track and a visible finish line. But nobody handed you the map. You're measuring yourself against a standard nobody actually gave you, on a track that was never fully laid. The shame is real. The timeline is not.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You look at where you are and compare it to where you think you should be by now — and there's this quiet, private weight that won't go away. You haven't told anyone because it sounds like weakness. But most men your age are carrying the exact same thing. The shame isn't evidence that you failed. It's evidence that you were given no map and still expected to arrive.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Write down the timeline you've been measuring yourself against. Then ask: whose plan is this, exactly — and did anyone who actually knows you help build it?
