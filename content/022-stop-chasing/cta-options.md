# CTA Options — Stop Chasing. Start Building.

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
One piece a week on building the kind of man who doesn't need to chase. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** Mirrors the article's central message exactly — "building the kind of man who doesn't need to chase" is both a description of the content and an articulation of the reader's desire. Direct and specific.

---

## CTA 2 — 30-Day Build Challenge

**The line:**
The experiment: stop chasing one thing for 30 days. Use that time and energy to build one thing instead. Track both. At the end of 30 days, compare the results. The data will tell you everything.

**Placement:** End of article, closing challenge

**Goal:** Behavioral commitment / 30-day personal experiment

**Tone note:** Framed as a personal experiment rather than a prescription — "the data will tell you everything" is scientific, not moralistic. Avoids telling the reader what to think; instead gives him the conditions to find out for himself. This lands better with skeptical men than "trust me, building is better."

---

## CTA 3 — Mission Clarity Workshop or Resource

**The line:**
The build starts with knowing what you're building. If that's unclear — if you don't yet have the mission that makes the chase unnecessary — that's the starting point. This is where we help with that.

**Placement:** Mid-article, after establishing that mission clarity is what the magnetic man has

**Goal:** Workshop, course, or coaching awareness focused on mission and purpose clarity

**Tone note:** Addresses the most common objection to the "stop chasing, start building" message: "I don't know what to build." Lands immediately after the article establishes that mission clarity is the foundation of the magnetic man's gravity. Not pushy — "this is where we help with that" is low-pressure and honest.
