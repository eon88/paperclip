# Social Posts — Week 45

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

A man who doesn't know who he is will use a relationship to find out. He'll make her responsible for whether he feels good about himself. That's not a relationship — that's a dependency. The work of becoming a good partner mostly happens before the relationship starts.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You want a great relationship. But if you're honest, you're not always sure what you'd bring to it. You have some things figured out. Other things are still a mess. You wonder if you're supposed to wait until you have it together, or if that day never comes. The answer is somewhere in the middle — and it starts with knowing honestly where you stand.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

What are you actually bringing to a relationship right now? Not what you plan to bring — what you're bringing today.
