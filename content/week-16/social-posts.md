# Social Posts — Week 16

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Comfort is not the goal. It never was. Your psychology was built for challenge, resistance, and difficulty — not as punishment, but as the actual mechanism by which men are formed. Frictionless lives produce hollow men. Your body has been waiting for you to give it something real to carry.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You have more comfort than any generation before you. And something still feels hollow. That's not ingratitude. That's your nervous system responding correctly to the wrong conditions — conditions it was never designed for. You weren't built to coast. You were built to carry.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Think of a man you genuinely respect — not admire from a distance, but actually respect. Was his character built through comfort or through difficulty? Now ask yourself: which one are you choosing?
