# Social Posts — Week 26

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Aspirations bend under pressure.

A code doesn't.

The difference: aspirations are who you want to be. A code is who you've decided to be — the principles you hold regardless of circumstances.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

Most men operate on borrowed values — principles absorbed from their environment without ever being examined or chosen.

They feel real. But they're a first draft written by other people.

A man who has never examined that draft can be moved off his foundation by pressure. The work of writing your own code is the work of becoming ungovernable by accident.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

What will you not do?

Not what do you aspire to — what is the line you will not cross, regardless of circumstances, regardless of what it costs you?

Name it. Write it down.
