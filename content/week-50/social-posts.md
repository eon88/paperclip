# Social Posts — Week 50

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Men don't quit because the failure was too large to recover from. They quit because the story they told about the failure made continuing feel pointless. The failure is not the problem. The narrative attached to it is. Choose a different interpretation and you change the outcome.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You tried something, and it didn't work. Maybe it failed publicly. Maybe quietly. And now there's a voice that says this proved something — that you don't have what it takes, that you were fooling yourself. You haven't fully shaken it. That voice is not the truth. It's just a story that was waiting for evidence. You get to tell a different story about the same event.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

What's the most honest interpretation of your last significant failure — not the self-indictment, and not the excuse — just what actually happened and what it means for what you do next?
