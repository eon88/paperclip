## Email Body

Here's the truth about your twenties:

They are the highest-leverage decade of your life.

Not because your life is over at 30 — it isn't. But because the decisions you make in your twenties — about your body, your mind, your skills, your relationships, your character — compound. And ten years of compounding in the right direction produces a man who stands on completely different ground.

The man at 20 is full of potential and largely unformed. That's not a criticism — it's the condition. He has the capacity to become almost anything. The question is what he does with it.

The man at 30 who used his twenties well is not dramatically different-looking. But he's operating from a fundamentally different foundation:

- His body has been trained for a decade — he's strong, capable, disciplined in a way that shows
- He has a real skill or two, built over years, that generates genuine income and options
- He has read enough to think clearly and broadly
- He has built or is actively building something — a business, a family, a mission
- He has been through enough difficulty to know what he's made of
- He knows what he values and has held those values under pressure

The man who coasted — who spent his twenties consuming, drifting, playing it comfortable — is also 30. And the gap between them is not talent. It is a decade of decisions.

**The compound question:** What are you building right now that your 30-year-old self will either thank you for or wish you'd started earlier?

Answer it honestly. Then start.
