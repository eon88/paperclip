## Post 1 — The core insight

Three circles: what you can do, what you care about, what the world needs.

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You've been trying to choose between work that pays and work that means something.

That's the wrong choice. The right question is: where do your skills, your caring, and real demand actually meet?

The answer exists. You find it by moving, not by sitting with the question.

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*
