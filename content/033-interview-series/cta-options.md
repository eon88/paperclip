# CTA Options — Men Worth Learning From: Interview Series Launch

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
Subscribe to receive each conversation as it's released. No algorithm. No noise. Just the conversation.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** "No algorithm. No noise." speaks to a specific frustration — the feeling that important content gets buried by platforms that favor engagement bait. The newsletter is positioned as the direct, unfiltered channel for the series. "Just the conversation" is clean and honest.

---

## CTA 2 — Guest Referral

**The line:**
If you know a man worth sitting down with — someone who's built something real, tells the truth, and has depth that comes from having paid the full price — send him our way. The best conversations start with an introduction.

**Placement:** End of article, closing call to action

**Goal:** Guest referral / community-sourced interview leads

**Tone note:** This CTA turns every reader into a potential source — which dramatically expands the reach for finding the right guests. "Paid the full price" is the specific criterion that distinguishes the kind of men this series is looking for from the typical interview subject. The framing ("the best conversations start with an introduction") is warm and true.

---

## CTA 3 — Community Membership

**The line:**
The conversations will be better with more people in the room asking the right questions. Join the community and help shape what we ask.

**Placement:** Mid-article or closing

**Goal:** Community membership

**Tone note:** Positions the community as active participants in the series — not just passive audience. "Help shape what we ask" gives members real ownership over the content direction. For men who want to be involved rather than just consume, this offer is compelling. It also creates genuine community investment in the series' success.
