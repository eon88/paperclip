## Email Body

The best education most men ever get isn't formal.

It's a conversation — at the right time, with the right man, who says exactly the thing you needed to hear. Not from a podcast, not from a polished panel. From a real conversation with a real person who's been through something real.

Most young men don't have access to that. Not because men worth learning from don't exist — they do. But because nobody has set up the room.

**We're setting up the room.**

Starting next week, we're launching a series of long-form conversations with men who have built something real — businesses, families, communities, bodies of work. Men who've failed seriously and rebuilt. Men who hold standards and can tell you what they've cost. Men who will answer the questions most people are afraid to ask.

The first conversation: a man who has built three businesses, closed one, buried his father, raised two sons, and has something specific to say about all of it. We're sitting down with him for two hours. You'll get the full conversation.

**What these conversations will cover:**
- How he found the thing worth building and what he walked away from to pursue it
- The hardest period of his life and what it made possible that ease couldn't have
- What he knows about relationships that he wishes someone had told him at 25
- The standard he holds himself to that most people around him don't
- What he'd do differently, with real specificity

No polish. No performance. Just the true version.

Come find out what it sounds like.

— The Digital Men Letter
