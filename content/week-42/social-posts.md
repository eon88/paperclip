## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

The lag is not a sign something is wrong. Every worthwhile thing has a gap between when you start doing the right things and when those things produce visible results. Men quit in that gap. The ones who don't are the ones who built something.

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You've been doing the work. Showing up, building habits, staying consistent. And nothing is showing yet. It feels like you're missing something — but what if you're not missing anything? What if you're just in the part of the story that doesn't get told?

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

Are you thinking about pivoting right now? Ask yourself this first: do you have new information that changes your direction, or are you just tired of waiting?
