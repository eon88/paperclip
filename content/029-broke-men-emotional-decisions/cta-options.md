# CTA Options — Broke Men Make Emotional Decisions

---

## CTA 1 — Newsletter Sign-Up

**The line:**
One piece a week on building the financial and mental clarity to make good decisions. Subscribe below.

**Placement:** End of article

**Goal:** Newsletter sign-up

**Tone note:** "Financial and mental clarity" combines both dimensions of the article — the money part and the decision-making part. Speaks to what the reader actually wants: not just more money, but clearer thinking and better outcomes.

---

## CTA 2 — Financial Runway Audit

**The line:**
Right now: open your bank account. Calculate how many months you could live at your current lifestyle without income. Write the number down. If it's under one — that number is your mission for the next 90 days. Everything else comes after.

**Placement:** End of article, closing challenge

**Goal:** Immediate financial self-audit / 90-day priority reset

**Tone note:** "Everything else comes after" is clear and demanding — it puts the buffer above the investment, the side hustle, the upgrade. The specific instruction (open the bank account, calculate the number) makes it executable in the next five minutes. No link needed.

---

## CTA 3 — Financial Framework or Course

**The line:**
If you want the full financial framework — from buffer to ownership to building real wealth — we put it together in one place. It's designed for men who are starting from scratch or starting over.

**Placement:** Mid-article, after the bad-deal / good-deal contrast

**Goal:** Course or financial education product awareness

**Tone note:** "Starting from scratch or starting over" is the key phrase — it removes the assumption that this is for men who are already financially stable. The man who read this article and recognized himself in the "takes the bad deal" scenario needs a starting point, not an advanced wealth-building framework. This CTA acknowledges where he actually is.
