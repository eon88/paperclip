# Social Posts — Week 36

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

Purpose is not a feeling you discover through enough introspection. It's a direction you build through action, skill, and the accumulation of being needed. The men who feel most purposeful didn't find the right answer. They picked a direction and kept moving.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You've been waiting for clarity about your purpose — some sign, some moment when it all becomes obvious. And nothing has come. So you wonder if you don't have one, or if you're missing something other people have. You're not. The problem is the definition. Purpose isn't a revelation. It's a direction you build.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

What does your existence currently make possible for someone else — specifically, concretely? If you can't answer that, that's where the work on purpose begins.
