# Social Posts — Week 39

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

The quality of your effort is a reflection of your character — not a reflection of the task.

The man who works the same way regardless of the stakes has made his effort unconditional.

That quality follows him everywhere.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You know the difference between doing work and doing work well.

One is presence in body. The other is presence everywhere — attention, care, a standard you hold even when nobody's watching.

Most days you do the first. Today try the second.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

What standard would you hold for your work if nobody was checking?

Hold that standard today — not for anyone watching. For what it builds in you.
