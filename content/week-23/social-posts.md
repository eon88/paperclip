# Social Posts — Week 23

---

## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: Twitter*

You cannot aim at a target you cannot see. Abstract goals — "be more disciplined," "handle things better" — are directions without destinations. Build a shelf of specific men who embody what you're aiming at. The specific is more powerful than the abstract. Always.

---

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You know roughly what kind of man you want to become. But when you try to picture it clearly, it gets blurry and abstract. That blurriness is the problem — you can't build something you can't picture. What you need isn't a better goal statement. You need examples. Real men whose specific lives give you something concrete to aim at.

---

## Post 3 — The challenge
*Best for: Twitter*

Don't put a man on your shelf because he's interesting or compelling or beautifully indifferent to rules. Put men on it whose lives, taken whole, you would actually want to aim at. Those are different lists. Which list is yours drawn from?
