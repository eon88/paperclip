## Post 1 — The core insight
*Best for: X (Twitter)*

A mission statement that doesn't help you say no to something is useless. If every option fits your mission, your mission isn't doing anything. One sentence that constrains your choices is worth more than a five-page vision document that decorates them.

## Post 2 — The named feeling
*Best for: Instagram*

You keep saying yes to things that look good on paper. The money is fine, the work is fine, the people are fine. But Sunday nights feel hollow and you can't explain why. That's not weakness — that's what it feels like to move without a destination.
