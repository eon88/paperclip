name: life-compass-content-engine
description: >
  Full content production workflow for The Digital Men Company. Takes one Life Compass
  topic and produces a complete content bundle: article, newsletter issue, and three
  social posts — ready for human review and publishing.
---

## How to use this skill

Provide:
- **Week number** (1–52)
- **Phase** — phase number and name
- **Topic** — the specific topic title from the Life Compass publishing schedule
- **CTA** (optional) — any specific next step or offer to include

## What this skill produces

A complete content bundle:
1. `article.md` — full 800–1200 word article
2. `newsletter.md` — Life Compass issue, ready to send
3. `social-posts.md` — three platform-ready posts

## The workflow

Run each step in order. Do not skip steps. Each step depends on the previous.

---

### Step 1 — Content Strategy
**Agent: Content Strategist**
**Skill: content-strategy**

Using the topic, phase, and week number, produce the full content plan:
- Audience pain (precise)
- Core insight
- Transformation
- 3 title options
- Outline (5–7 sections)
- Opening hook
- CTA direction

Present the plan and wait for title selection before proceeding.

---

### Step 2 — Write the Article
**Agent: Writer**
**Skill: write-article**

Using the approved content plan, write the full article.
- Follow the outline exactly
- Start with the opening hook
- 800–1200 words
- Every sentence earns its place

---

### Step 3 — Edit the Article
**Agent: Editor**
**Skill: edit-article**

Edit the draft:
- Cut the fluff
- Tighten the language
- Strengthen opening and ending
- Check flow
- Mission alignment check

Output: `article.md`

---

### Step 4 — Write the Newsletter
**Agent: Newsletter Writer**
**Skill: write-newsletter**

Adapt the edited article into the Life Compass newsletter format:
- Subject line
- Direct-address body (400–600 words)
- This week's anchor
- Closing

Output: `newsletter.md`

---

### Step 5 — Create Social Posts
**Agent: Social Repurposer**

From the article and newsletter, produce three social posts:
- Post 1: the core insight
- Post 2: the named feeling
- Post 3: the challenge or question

Output: `social-posts.md`

---

### Step 6 — Package for Review
**Agent: CEO**

Compile the full bundle and present for human review:

```
Life Compass — Issue [number]
Phase [number]: [phase name]
Topic: [topic title]

FILES READY FOR REVIEW:
- article.md
- newsletter.md
- social-posts.md

NOTES: [any mission alignment flags or editorial notes]
```

Human approval required before publishing.
