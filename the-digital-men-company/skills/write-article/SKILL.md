## How to use this skill

Provide the full content plan from the content-strategy skill:
- Audience pain
- Core insight
- Transformation
- Chosen title
- Outline
- Opening hook
- CTA direction

## What this skill produces

A full article, 800–1200 words, structured as follows:

### Opening (use the hook from the plan)
The first paragraph must make the reader feel understood before asking anything of them. Start with the opening hook from the content plan. Do not warm up. Do not introduce yourself. Start in the middle of something true.

### Body sections (follow the outline)
Work through each section in order. For each section:
- Deliver its stated purpose fully
- Develop one idea — do not pile multiple points together
- Include at least one concrete example, image, or specific detail that makes the idea real
- Keep paragraphs short: 2-4 sentences

### Closing
Do not summarize. A summary tells the reader what they just read. A conclusion gives them something to carry forward. End with a statement or image that lands with weight and sends the man back into his life with something.

### CTA (from the plan)
One or two sentences at the end. Natural, direct. Not salesy. Points the reader to the next step that genuinely serves him.

## Quality checks before finishing
- Does the opening hook immediately? Would a distracted man keep reading?
- Does every section deliver exactly what the outline said it would?
- Is there any sentence that restates what was already said? Cut it.
- Is there any generic self-help language? ("reach your potential", "level up", "the best version of yourself") Replace with specific, concrete language.
- Does the voice stay consistent throughout? Clear, direct, grounded — not preachy, not corporate, not fluffy.
- Does the ending land?

## Voice rules
- Clear, strong, direct, grounded
- No filler transitions
- No restating what was already said
- No generic self-help language
- Treat the reader as capable
- 800–1200 words — every word earns its place
