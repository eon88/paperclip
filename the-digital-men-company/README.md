# The Digital Men Company

> A content company helping young men move from confusion to clarity, calling, order, and contribution.

## What this is

Most young men today feel a vague sense that something is missing. They can feel the potential inside them but they have no map to reach it. The map used to be passed down invisibly — through proximity to other men doing hard things. That chain is broken.

The Digital Men Company rebuilds the map. It produces the **Life Compass** newsletter — 52 weekly issues guiding a young man through a complete journey from quiet desperation to purposeful living.

## Install

```bash
npx companies.sh add eon88/companies/the-digital-men-company
```

## Agents

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| CEO | Orchestrates production, maintains mission alignment |
| Content Strategist | Turns topics into full content plans |
| Writer | Writes the full article from the content plan |
| Editor | Sharpens, tightens, and checks mission alignment |
| Newsletter Writer | Adapts articles into Life Compass email issues |
| Social Repurposer | Extracts core ideas into three social posts |

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|-------|-------------|
| `life-compass-content-engine` | Full workflow: topic → article + newsletter + social posts |
| `content-strategy` | Turns a topic into a complete content plan |
| `write-article` | Writes a full article from a content plan |
| `edit-article` | Edits a draft for clarity, strength, and mission alignment |
| `write-newsletter` | Adapts an article into a Life Compass newsletter issue |

## Run the content engine

```text
Use the life-compass-content-engine skill.
Week: 1
Phase: Phase 1 — The Quiet Desperation
Topic: The Feeling You Can't Shake — Why Something Always Feels Missing
```

## The 52-week schedule

8 phases. A complete journey.

- **Phase 1** — The Quiet Desperation (Weeks 1–7)
- **Phase 2** — The Broken Transmission (Weeks 8–15)
- **Phase 3** — How Men Are Actually Built (Weeks 16–21)
- **Phase 4** — Building Your Own Template (Weeks 22–28)
- **Phase 5** — Foundations (Weeks 29–35)
- **Phase 6** — Purpose and Work (Weeks 36–42)
- **Phase 7** — People and Contribution (Weeks 43–49)
- **Phase 8** — The Long Game (Weeks 50–52)

Full schedule in `skills/life-compass-content-engine/SKILL.md`.

## Output per issue

Each run of the content engine produces:
- `article.md` — 800–1200 word article
- `newsletter.md` — Life Compass email issue, ready to send
- `social-posts.md` — three platform-ready posts

Human approval required before publishing.
